Cut vs fill: where the proposed surface (subgrade) is below the existing ground, the earth between them is cut; where it is above, the space between them is fill. The existing line is the top of what is there -- existing asphalt in a cut is dug out with the earth (counted as cut), under a fill it stays and the fill is built on it (not counted); poles change nothing.
